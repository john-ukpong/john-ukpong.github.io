About Me

People often ask why I moved from seven years of client relations into Salesforce administration. The truth is simple; I love systems that solve problems. I spent years navigating complex workflows and dealing with manual bottlenecks, and I realized I'd rather be the person building the solution than the person struggling with the workaround.

I moved to Japan in 2023. I am a Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator, but I am a problem-solver first. Whether I am designing a new object relationship, building a flow that eliminates manual data entry, or creating a dashboard that makes a messy pipeline clear, my goal is to make the system work for the person using it.

When I am not in the Org, you can find me on the futsal pitch, catching a match, or digging into JavaScript to sharpen my technical edge. I believe that whether it is on the field or in a developer environment, if you are not constantly practicing, you are standing still.

I treat my toolkit like a craftsman treats their gear. I am deep into the Salesforce ecosystem, but I am currently expanding into web technologies like JavaScript to build even more custom, integrated experiences.

If you are looking for someone who understands both the human side of operations and the technical side of platform administration, let's talk. I am based in Japan and open to remote opportunities where I can help a team get a little more efficient every day.
